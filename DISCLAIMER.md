## Nature of the References: Paraphrase, Commentary, and Original Illustration

Where Marvel scenes are referenced, the following applies without exception:

1. **Paraphrase only.** Every scene description is an original prose retelling, written from scratch. No line of dialogue is quoted verbatim. The language, structure, and expression are entirely the authors' own.

2. **Original illustrations.** All visual representations of scenes or concepts are procedurally generated, abstract, original artworks created in code (SVG/Canvas). They are not derived from, traced from, or substantially similar to any frame of any Marvel production.

3. **Commentary and interpretation.** The references constitute commentary and critical interpretation — identifying a scene as an illustrative example of a philosophical or moral concept (e.g. courage, renewal, fearlessness) and inviting discussion of that concept. This is a form of educational commentary, not reproduction.

4. **User-supplied access.** Users wishing to view the actual scenes are directed to locate them independently on licensed platforms (such as Disney+). The application provides only a search phrase to assist that independently undertaken activity. The application derives no commercial benefit from this direction.

## Educational and Non-Commercial Purpose

*Nine Nights* is designed exclusively for personal, family, and educational use. It is offered free of charge. No revenue is generated from it. Its purpose is to help families explore traditional Hindu philosophical and devotional stories — drawn from source texts including the Devi Mahatmya, the Rig Veda, the Devi Bhagavata Purana, and the Yoga Sutras — and to connect those ideas to narratives that younger audiences may recognise from contemporary film. The Marvel references serve solely as comparative discussion prompts; the project has no relationship, commercial or otherwise, with the Marvel franchise.
